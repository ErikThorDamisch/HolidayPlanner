How Holiday Planner works
📅 Calendar

The whole year at a glance. Click any working day to book time off — as a full day, morning or afternoon. The bar at the top shows how many of your days you've used and how many are left.

🏝️ Booking & ranges

To plan a longer trip, open any day and use Date Range — it fills every working day between two dates and automatically skips free weekends and public holidays. Consecutive days are grouped into one vacation.

🧳 My Vacations

Every planned trip listed with its days and hours. Use the download icon on a trip to export just that vacation, or the × to remove it.

⚙️ Settings

Public holidays: load Zürich or Hamburg holidays in one click, or add your own (full or ½ day). Work settings: set your yearly vacation budget and daily hours. Export: download all vacations, or all holidays, as an .ics file for Google / Outlook / Apple Calendar.

🗓️ Weekend working days

If you regularly work Saturdays or Sundays, mark them in Settings as a half or full working day. They then become bookable and count towards your budget (a half day counts as 0.5).

➕ Compensation days

Worked on a free weekend as a one-off? Click that Saturday/Sunday and choose +1 or +0.5. The compensation day is added straight to your vacation balance for the year.
